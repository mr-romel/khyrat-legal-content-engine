Khyrat Legal Content Engine

Khyrat Legal Content Engine is a content workflow tool designed to help its operator prepare and publish legal-information content to connected publishing platforms, including Blogger.

The Blogger integration uses Google OAuth to authorize actions on a Blogger account selected by the account holder. Access is used to provide the publishing features requested by the operator.
